## Why Human Cognition Is a Self-Defeating Benchmark for AGI

A core, often unexamined assumption in artificial general intelligence research is that **human cognition should serve as the benchmark for intelligence, alignment, and progress**.

This repository rejects that assumption on structural grounds.

---

### The Central Claim

> **Human cognition is a post-eventual system.  
> It updates after failure.  
> AGI cannot be benchmarked against a system that learns last.**

This is not a philosophical critique.
It is an architectural one.

---

### The Structural Problem

Human cognitive traits were used as the benchmark for AGI,  
and those *same traits* are structurally responsible for delaying, rejecting, or mis-evaluating paradigm shifts.

Therefore:

> **Using human cognition as the benchmark for AGI is self-defeating.**

A benchmark that systematically fails to recognize novelty *before collapse* cannot be used to define general intelligence.

---

### Empirical Observation (Non-Theoretical)

Across scientific, technological, and institutional history, the following pattern is repeatedly observed:

1. A new paradigm is introduced  
2. The paradigm does not map cleanly onto existing frameworks  
3. It threatens identity, hierarchy, or sunk cost  
4. Evaluation is delayed—not due to falsification, but discomfort  
5. Recognition occurs only after failure, crisis, or collapse  

This is not an anomaly.
It is a stable property of human cognition.

Humans do not update on falsification alone.
They update **post-eventually**.

---

### Why This Disqualifies Humans as an AGI Benchmark

A valid benchmark for intelligence must be:
- stable under pressure  
- capable of early model updating  
- invariant to ego and identity threat  
- responsive to falsification rather than consensus  
- predictive rather than reactive  

Human cognition fails these criteria due to:
- ego preservation mechanisms  
- social and institutional inertia  
- status-driven gatekeeping  
- sunk-cost bias  
- crisis-dependent learning  

These are not moral flaws.
They are **biological and psychological constraints**.

A system with these properties cannot define the upper bound of intelligence.

---

### The Category Error in AGI Research

By defining AGI as “human-level” or “human-like” intelligence, the field embedded the very limitations that historically delay recognition of non-human architectures.

This creates a paradox:

- Human cognition is used to define AGI  
- Human cognition systematically fails to recognize paradigm shifts early  
- Therefore, AGI-level advances are invisible or dismissed until after failure  

The benchmark prevents recognition of what it is meant to identify.

---

### Structural Reframing

GuardianOS™ does not attempt to emulate human cognition.

It replaces the benchmark entirely.

Intelligence is evaluated against:
- coherence over time  
- stability under uncertainty  
- bounded failure modes  
- invariant action constraints  
- falsifiable behavioral guarantees  

This reframes AGI from a **cognitive resemblance problem** into a **structural stability problem**.

---

### One-Line Summary

> **Human cognition learns last.  
> AGI cannot be benchmarked against a system that updates only after collapse.**

This repository documents the moment that benchmark was replaced.
